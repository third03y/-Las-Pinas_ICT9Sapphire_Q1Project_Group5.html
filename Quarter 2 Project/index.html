Welcome to OBMC

A place to learn, lead, and belong.

OB Montessori Center (OBMC) is a Philippine school community inspired by the Montessori approach to education. Learners are encouraged to take an active role in their growth, build practical skills, and show respect for themselves and others.

This student-made page introduces the Las Piñas campus, gives an overview of school levels, and shares our group's ICT project. For official admissions details, please contact the school directly.
